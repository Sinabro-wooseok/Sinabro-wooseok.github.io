Dock Ferry Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Dock Ferry ("the game") is developed by Wooseok Lee, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device

Your level, coins and harbor decorations are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

Advertising

The mobile app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

The web version on CrazyGames shows ads provided by CrazyGames under its own privacy policy: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
